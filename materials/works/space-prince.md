# Space Prince

*2026, in progress · Digital game*

The idea for *Space Prince* came to me at a gallery opening on the Lower East Side in January 2026.
The director of Artist Commons told me the program was starting on Monday and asked what I’d do if I joined.
It was a Friday.
I knew almost immediately what I wanted to try.
A time and a place determine a birth chart.
What if that chart could be your character sheet and your save file?
You could play as yourself, or as someone who lived centuries ago.

I’d been playing *Loot Survivor 2* by Provable Games.
I admired how much of a dungeon you could get out of simple relationships among weapons, gear, and monsters, all within the constraints of running a game on a blockchain.
A beast you defeated in the dungeon could even become a collectible you took away with you.
I was already interested in what a player could carry out of a game.
What caught my attention here was how much play a few well-chosen rules could sustain.

I spent the next day working through the idea in a conversation with ChatGPT.
My first sketch proposed rock-paper-scissors combat among astrological signs or elements and left progression wide open.
I had a lot of respect for astrology as a symbolic system.
One ambition I still have is to make the mechanics correspond closely enough to astrology that someone comes away knowing how to read something in a chart because they’ve learned to play.

Hermann Hesse’s *Siddhartha* has helped me think about what kind of play I want.
What stayed with me was how the time Siddhartha spent as an ascetic, in the city, and by the river all went into the person he became.
In my own life I think about how to respond to a situation with a little more skill and grace, and how to notice the effect I’m having on someone else.
That’s the quality of attention I’d like an encounter in the game to reward.

The game’s score is called Light, and a run leaves a star in the prince’s sky.
I’m still working out how to earn that star in a way that feels true to the ambition.
I’d like a player to be able to point one out to somebody else and remember a situation they navigated well.
