# Infinite Knot

*2009–2012 · Paint*

*Infinite Knot* is a series of three murals, painted in three student co-op houses.
Each is a Tibetan Buddhist endless knot: one band that turns at right angles and passes over and under itself.

The form is borrowed, and it is drawn by rule.
Each space is measured and an even grid of dots laid out across it.
The band is ruled through the dots in a fixed pattern of over and under.

The craft was in fitting a known form to each space.
Once the pencil lines were down, painting was coloring inside them.
It became a kind of meditation, which suits the subject.

In the co-ops, painting a mural was a popular way to contribute to house culture.
Drawing them became a kind of spiritual practice.

The later *Identity Matrix* would follow a strange rule of its own.
