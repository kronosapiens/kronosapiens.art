# Identity Matrix

*2019 · Spray paint and stencil*

*Identity Matrix* is three stencils repeated in a three-by-three grid, sprayed in orange, fuchsia, and teal on the wall of a rooftop deck.
The figures are three views of the same person: a face in profile, a face in portrait, and a distant figure holding what might be balloons.

Each figure takes two of the three colors.
Every figure appears once in each row and column, and so does every pairing of colors, as in Sudoku.
Every figure also appears once in every pairing: nine combinations, exactly enough to fill the grid, with no image repeated and no image absent.

The craft was in working out a pattern more than in composing an image.
From row to row the figures rotate one place and the pairings rotate the other way.

The image follows from the pattern: every row and column holds each color twice.
A person can enjoy the wall without noticing any of this.

In linear algebra, the identity matrix is a grid of numbers that returns whatever it is given.
This one is a matrix about a person.

*Space Prince* applies the same thinking to a larger system, where the rules of a game show a player their birth chart.
