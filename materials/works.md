# Selected works by Daniel Kronovet

Each title links to that work’s page, which has the full description and the documentation links.

## [Space Prince](works/space-prince.md)

*2026, in progress · Digital game*

A game you play as a birth chart.
A time and a place fix the positions of the Sun, the Moon, and five planets, and that chart is the player’s character sheet, game board, and save file.
Every run leaves a star in the player’s sky.

## [The Lost Temple Adventure](works/the-lost-temple-adventure.md)

*2025 · Onchain game and in-person treasure hunt*

A treasure hunt run for a week at the Devconnect conference in Buenos Aires.
Players won games on their phones and solved physical puzzles around the venue.
Each solution drew another stretch of the route on a digital map that was theirs to keep.

## [Ronin’s Pact](works/ronins-pact.md)

*2025 · Onchain game*

A digital badge that records three trials of technique, wisdom, and spirit.
It starts as an empty sword rack bearing the player’s name, and each completed trial places a sword on it.

## [Identity Matrix](works/identity-matrix.md)

*2019 · Spray paint and stencil*

Three figures found in a page of doodles, stenciled on a rooftop wall in every pairing of three colors.
No figure and no pairing repeats in any row or column of the three-by-three grid.

## [Infinite Knot](works/infinite-knot.md)

*2009 · Paint*

A Tibetan Buddhist endless knot painted on a door in a Berkeley student co-op.
The band was ruled onto a measured grid before any paint went on.
