## Space Prince

*2026, in progress · Digital game*

Each prince begins with a time and a place.
Together they determine a birth chart that serves as the character sheet.
When two charts meet, the player chooses a planet to send into the encounter, and its effects travel along the lines connecting planets in the other chart.
Each run leaves a star in the prince’s sky.

[Source code](https://github.com/kronosapiens/SpacePrince)

## The Lost Temple Adventure

*2025 · Onchain game and in-person treasure hunt*

At Devconnect in Argentina, players followed a treasure hunt through the conference, alternating between onchain games and puzzles they could solve in person.
One puzzle asked them to put a stack of Pokémon cards in order using a type chart, while another asked them to interpet a mid-90's living room to deduce a classic video game code.
Each player carried a digital map with its own arrangement of waypoints, and each solved challenge revealed another stretch of the route.
After playing the treasure hunt, their adventure map became a permanent digital collectible.

[Live game](https://adventure.cartridge.gg/) · [Source code](https://github.com/cartridge-gg/adventure) · [Gameplay video](https://www.dropbox.com/scl/fi/13p1rf6j225kacf64u5ed/Lost_Temple_demo.MOV?rlkey=5hdq7lfprevt3ibux014hhujc&dl=0) · [Performance retrospective](https://github.com/cartridge-gg/adventure/blob/main/retro/RETRO.md)

## Ronin’s Pact

*2025 · Onchain game*

The player starts with an image of an empty sword rack bearing their name.
The player undertakes trials of technique, wisdom, and spirit.
Completing a trial places its sword on the rack.
When all three swords are in place, the background lights up.

[Live game](https://ronin.cartridge.gg/) · [Source code](https://github.com/cartridge-gg/ronins-pact)

## Identity Matrix

*2019 · Spray paint and stencil*

Small stenciled signs sit in rows on a white exterior wall beneath a barred window.
Teal, magenta, and orange strokes cross at right angles, with four-pointed stars in the spaces between them.
In the photograph, shadows from overhead wires cut diagonally across the grid.

[Photograph](https://www.instagram.com/p/B2G_GGfBRI8/)

## Infinite Knot

*2009 · Paint*

A dark blue band crosses the face of a yellow door, turning at right angles and passing over and under itself.
A wooden brace cuts across the lower part of the knot.
